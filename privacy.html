Privacy policy

Last updated 26 November 2026

At a glance

  • No account, no sign-in. You never give us your name, email or phone number.
  • Your figures stay on your phone. We have no server that receives your wealth, payments or donations.
  • The app goes online for three things only: prices, ads in the free version, and the Pro purchase.
  • We never sell your data, and we never see your card details.

ZakatEase is a Zakat, Fitrana and Sadaqah calculator for Android, made by Waseem Hassan in Pakistan. This policy explains what data the app uses, where it goes and the choices you have. It applies to the ZakatEase app on Google Play.

Data that stays on your phone

Everything you enter is stored only on your device. This includes your amounts, gold and silver, Zakat payments, donations and who they went to, Fitrana details, family profiles and settings.

  • There is no account and nothing is uploaded to us.
  • Backup files are made only when you choose to make one, and you decide where to save them (for example Google Drive). They are never sent to us.
  • PDF reports are created on your phone and shared only where you send them.
  • Reminders and price notifications are scheduled on your phone.

These outside services receive some data when the app uses them. Each has its own privacy policy.

ZakatEase Pro removes ads. With Pro, the app no longer requests ads from Google AdMob.

Ads and consent

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings, Ad privacy choices.

Your choices

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings, Google, Ads).
  • Change ad consent in the app's settings.
  • Delete all your app data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it. Because we hold no copy, this removes it completely, apart from any backup files you saved yourself.

Children

ZakatEase is meant for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Security

All connections the app makes use encrypted HTTPS. Your records are protected by Android's app storage, which other apps cannot read.
